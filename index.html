About us

A university built around real-world skills

Marwadi University is a private university in Rajkot, Gujarat. Its approach blends strong academics with projects, internships and industry interaction, so graduates are ready for work from day one.

  • Practical, outcome-based curriculum
  • Experienced faculty and industry mentors
  • Active placement and career support
  • Clubs, events and a vibrant student life

Our vision

To nurture capable, ethical and innovative graduates who contribute to society and industry.
